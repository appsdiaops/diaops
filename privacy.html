CoreOps Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 6, 2026
Last updated: March 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Diaops llc, 3534 Lovage Drive, Apex, NC 27502 (“Diaops,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when providing CoreOps. CoreOps is a workforce and business-operations platform for scheduling, attendance, timekeeping, employee communications, inventory, human-resources workflows, and analytics.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, username, email address, telephone number, profile photograph, and uploaded files.
  • Verification and preferences: email and phone verification status, SMS consent status, email-notification choices, and other communication preferences.
  • Consent records: the date and time, IP address, user, phone number, consent source and method, and disclosure version associated with consent.
  • Messaging records: message content, SMS delivery status, Twilio message ID, delivery errors, and replies such as STOP or HELP.
  • Workforce and operations information: schedules, schedule changes, attendance, clock-in, clock-out, break, location, manager-review, and related workplace records.
  • Technical and security information: device and browser type, IP address, cookies, application activity, authentication events, security events, diagnostics, and audit logs.

Your employer or other organization administering CoreOps may provide information about you and determine which workplace records are processed. We also receive information directly from you, automatically from your use of CoreOps, and from contracted providers.

2. How we use information

Diaops llc uses information to:

  • authenticate users and verify email addresses and telephone numbers;
  • deliver verification codes and schedule and attendance notifications;
  • maintain employee communication preferences;
  • process SMS opt-outs and HELP requests;
  • monitor delivery and troubleshoot failed messages;
  • manage schedules, attendance, timekeeping, breaks, and authorized workplace workflows;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • maintain compliance, security, and audit records; and
  • operate, support, analyze, and improve CoreOps.

3. Twilio, service providers, and disclosure

CoreOps uses Twilio as a messaging service provider. Twilio processes telephone numbers, message content, and delivery information only as necessary to transmit and manage text messages on behalf of CoreOps.

CoreOps does not sell or rent mobile telephone numbers, SMS consent records, or messaging information for advertising or marketing purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Contracted processors such as Twilio may process information solely to provide the messaging service; this limited processing is not a disclosure of opt-in data or consent for those providers’ own marketing. Other vendors may process hosting, security, analytics, storage, and support information under contractual restrictions. We may also disclose non-SMS information to your employer or authorized CoreOps administrators, to comply with law or legal process, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction. We do not use SMS opt-in data or consent for advertising.

4. Cookies and application analytics

CoreOps may use necessary cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember settings, secure accounts, understand feature performance, diagnose errors, and improve the application. Browser controls may limit cookies, but disabling necessary cookies can prevent parts of CoreOps from working.

5. Who may access employee information

Access is limited according to role and business need. Your employer’s authorized administrators and managers may access workforce information relevant to their responsibilities. Authorized Diaops llc personnel and contracted processors may access information when needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, support, or comply with legal obligations for CoreOps. Your employer may have its own privacy notices and legal duties for information it controls.

6. Retention

We retain records for as long as required for operations, security, audit, legal, and compliance purposes. Retention varies by record type, the applicable customer relationship, legal requirements, and the need to investigate disputes or security events. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to backups and legal obligations.

7. Security safeguards

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls, authentication, logging, and protections for data in transit and at rest where appropriate. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You may review or update certain account and communication settings in CoreOps. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting your employer’s CoreOps administrator or admin@diaops.com. Requests may be subject to identity verification, your employer’s instructions, legal exceptions, and retention duties.

SMS: SMS consent is optional. Reply STOP to opt out of all SMS categories at any time. Reply HELP for assistance or email admin@diaops.com. SMS cannot be enabled until your phone number is verified. Opting out does not stop authorized workplace communications through CoreOps, email, or another channel.

Email: Where available, manage optional email notifications in CoreOps or use instructions in the email. Operational, security, or account messages may still be sent when necessary.

State rights: Residents of some U.S. states may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to appeal a denied request, subject to applicable exceptions. We do not discriminate for exercising applicable rights. Where Diaops llc processes employee data for an employer, we may direct the request to that employer.

9. Children’s privacy

CoreOps is a workplace service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate.

10. Policy changes

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in CoreOps, law, or our practices. We will revise the “Last updated” date and provide additional notice of material changes when required.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email admin@diaops.com or write to: Diaops llc, 3534 Lovage Drive, Apex, NC 27502.
