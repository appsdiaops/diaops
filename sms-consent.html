How consent is recorded

In CoreOps, SMS consent is separate from email preferences and general terms acceptance. On save, CoreOps records the disclosure version, user, verified phone number, timestamp, IP address, and consent source. A STOP reply disables every SMS category; HELP returns assistance information. Users can opt out without losing access to CoreOps.
